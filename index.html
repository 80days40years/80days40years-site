The rules

  • No flying. Not once, not even a short hop.
  • Land and sea only. Trains, ships, buses, boats and feet.
  • Eighty days. Out on 2 October, back in London by 21 December.
  • One person a day. Eighty people met on the way round, from ships' captains to station cooks.
